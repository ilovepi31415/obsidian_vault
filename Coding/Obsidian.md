## Callouts
Use `>[!<type>] <optional title>` to create a callout that stands out from the rest of the text

>[!info] Like this
>woah i did a callout

Callouts can be made foldable by adding a `+` or `-` after the type. The choice determines whether the callout defaults to folded or unfolded
>[!faq]- Folded
>hello

>[!faq]+ Unfolded
>goodbye

Callouts can also be nested
>[!Abstract] First Layer
>>[!Example] Second Layer
